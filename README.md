If your goal is to get freelance clients, don't build "another SaaS dashboard" or "another agency website." Build projects that make a client immediately think:

> "This developer already understands my business."

Here are 3 highly specific portfolio projects.

# 1. Commercial Solar Installation ROI Platform

### Target Clients

Solar installers, renewable energy consultants, energy auditors.

### Problem

Most solar companies still use PDFs, Excel sheets, WhatsApp, and phone calls to estimate savings.

### Build

A web app where a business owner can:

* Enter monthly electricity bill
* Select city
* Enter roof size
* Choose commercial or residential
* Get projected solar savings
* ROI timeline
* Carbon reduction estimate
* Download proposal PDF

### Features

* Interactive ROI calculator
* Map-based roof location selection
* Proposal generation
* Lead capture CRM
* Admin dashboard
* Email automation

### Portfolio Impact

Shows:

* Advanced forms
* Calculations
* PDF generation
* Dashboard UI
* Maps integration
* Business logic

A local solar company could realistically buy this.

# 2. Construction Tender Intelligence Platform

## If I were building a portfolio specifically to win high-paying freelance work as a WordPress/web developer:

1. Agricultural Equipment Rental Marketplace
2. Commercial Solar ROI Platform
3. Construction Tender Intelligence Platform

The reason is simple: all three solve expensive business problems. Business owners don't pay well for pretty websites; they pay for software that saves time, generates leads, or increases revenue.

A strong strategy is to build each project as if it were for a real company:

* Real branding
* Real copywriting
* Real data
* Mobile-first design
* Production-quality UI
* Case study explaining the business problem, process, and outcome

That makes your portfolio look like client work instead of practice projects.
